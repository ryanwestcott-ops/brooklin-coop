Time: Approx. 10 minutes.

Respond first. Record facts second.

  1. Get safe and get help. Stop the task, avoid further danger and follow the emergency plan. Call 911 when needed. Do not attempt an untrained rescue or cleanup.
  2. Tell the workplace supervisor promptly. Report injuries, exposure concerns, near misses and unsafe instructions—even if they seem minor.
  3. Tell your co-op teacher promptly. If the teacher is unavailable, contact the school office or administration. Do not wait for a daily log in an urgent situation.
  4. Give facts privately. State when, where, the task, what happened, who was told and what help was requested.

A daily reflection is learning evidence. It is not an urgent incident report.

Time: Approx. 10 minutes.

Know your reporting role.

The Work Education Agreement identifies the placement and coverage arrangement. It must be completed before placement. Your teacher confirms the applicable arrangement.

  • Student: report promptly, provide accurate facts and cooperate with the process.
  • School-board representative: submits the required WSIB documents for a Ministry-covered placement.
  • Placement host: handles employer reporting when it provides coverage.
  • WSIB: determines entitlement using the facts and applicable policy.

Form 7 is not student homework. Report what happened; do not decide whether something is covered.

WSIB: pupils in work education programs · Ontario PPM 76A

Time: Approx. 50 minutes.

Two-minute misconception check.

Correct both statements: “Nobody was hurt, so I do not need to report it” and “I complete Form 7 myself.” Then identify your reporting contact and backup.

Time: Approx. 20 minutes.

Your day in CO-OP Desk.

Arrive

Scan the placement QR and tap Check in. Use your DDSB Google account. Location sharing is optional.

Leave

Scan again, answer the structured question, write a short reflection, and tap Check out. Hours are calculated automatically.

If checkout is missed

At 5:00 the system closes the day using estimated hours and emails you. Tell the teacher the actual times. Do not knowingly sign an incorrect record. Confirm any approved shift extending beyond 5:00 in advance.

Friday

Read the week’s daily entries and total hours before signing. The order is student → supervisor → teacher. Report errors before signing. Your week’s daily reflections are the reflection record; there is no separate weekly essay.

2 hours 30 minutes = 2.5 hours, not 2.30. Record actual approved time. Do not add commute time or change a short day to reach 2.5 hours.

Time: Approx. 15 minutes.

Communicate before a small problem grows.

  • Absent: use the teacher-provided absence link, notify the placement and follow school attendance procedures.
  • Late: notify the required people before the shift when possible; give facts and an expected arrival time.
  • Untrained task: pause and request instruction and supervision.
  • Privacy: keep client names, records, identifying images and confidential information out of logs, social media and public AI tools.

Your real QR and absence link come from the teacher. Do not use a real QR to practise today.
