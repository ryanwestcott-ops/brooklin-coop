Know your logs

Use your DDSB Google account. Keep the CO-OP Desk instructions on page 11 of your booklet handy.

Arrive → Check in

At your placement, scan its QR poster, allow location and tap Check in. Check in when you arrive. If your phone, account or location is not working, tell your supervisor and Mr. Westcott.

Leave → Reflect → Check out

Scan again. Answer the day’s question, write a short reflection (about three sentences), then tap Check out. Your log is required to check out. Record your actual time; do not include travel to and from placement.

What makes a useful daily reflection?

What did I do? What did I learn? What will I try next? Be specific. Leave out client names, private records and identifying details.

Example: “I helped sort supplies with my supervisor. I learned to check the item code instead of guessing from the packaging. Tomorrow I will practise using the checklist more independently.”

This practice is not saved or submitted. Do not scan a real placement QR to practise today.

Friday → Read → Sign

Open your weekly email. Review the daily logs and total hours, then sign as the student. Your supervisor signs next, followed by your teacher. Report incorrect hours before signing.

Absent, late or forgot to check out?
  • Absent or late: contact your placement yourself and use CO-OP Desk’s reporting process. Report planned absences early, following your booklet’s instructions. If something unexpected happens, report it as soon as possible.
  • The app does not notify your placement for you.
  • Missed checkout: estimated hours need review. Explain what happened and give Mr. Westcott your actual times.

Quick check: Can you explain the two daily scans and who signs your week? If not, reread the steps or ask Mr. Westcott.

Time: Approx. 80 minutes, as needed.

First-day questions

Open the answers you need. Bring any unanswered placement questions to Mr. Westcott.

Where do I go, when do I arrive and what do I wear?

Use the arrangements confirmed in your booklet: address, entrance, supervisor, start and finish times, clothing and footwear. Check your travel plan and leave a buffer. Ask about anything still marked “to confirm.”

What if I do not know how to do a task?

Pause and ask your supervisor to explain or demonstrate. Do not use unfamiliar equipment or products without the required instruction and supervision.

What if something feels unsafe or I am injured?

Stop, get to safety and tell your supervisor. Get help and follow the workplace emergency plan. Contact Mr. Westcott promptly; do not wait for your daily reflection to report a concern.

What if I finish my work?

Ask your supervisor what approved task you should do next. Show interest, ask questions and stay within the tasks you have been taught.

What if I am nervous or need help?

You are there to learn. Ask questions and give yourself time to settle in. Message Mr. Westcott through Remind for routine questions. For urgent help, contact your supervisor and school directly rather than waiting for a message reply.
