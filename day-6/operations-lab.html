Build your contact map.

Use roles, not private phone numbers. Your actual names and contact methods stay in the teacher-approved location.

Station 25–15 minutes

Run the incident desk.

A · Small cut

You cut a finger while unpacking stock. Someone says to finish first.

B · Exposure

An unfamiliar bodily-fluid spill is near your work area. You are not trained to handle it.

C · Near miss

A poorly stored item falls beside you. Nobody appears hurt.

D · Unfamiliar machine

Your regular supervisor is away. Another worker tells you to use a machine you have never used.

E · Travel question

Compare an ordinary commute incident with one during an approved assigned delivery. Someone asks which is “definitely covered.”

F · Delayed symptoms

After leaving, you notice symptoms that may relate to the day. You reported nothing before leaving.
