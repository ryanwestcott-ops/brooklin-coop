PRACTICE WEEK — FICTIONAL, NOT AN OFFICIAL LOG
Use these examples to check totals, not to replace CO-OP Desk. For this exercise only, the listed excluded breaks do not count and ordinary commute time does not count. Do not assume these examples define every placement's break arrangements.

Mon: arrived 1:00, left 3:30; no excluded break. Calculate counted hours.
Tue: arrived 12:45, left 3:45; 30-minute excluded break. Calculate counted hours.
Wed: arrived 1:00, left 3:15; no excluded break. The record says 2.5 hours. Give the actual hours and next action. Do not change actual time to meet the minimum.
Thu: approved absence; no attendance. Give the counted hours and required notifications.
Fri: arrived 1:00, left 3:45; no excluded break. The system's estimated record says 4 hours. Give the actual hours and next action.
1. Calculate the corrected weekly total in hours/minutes AND decimal hours. This shortened fictional week is an error-checking exercise, not an approved attendance pattern.
2. Explain why 2:30 hours and 2.30 decimal hours are different.
3. Before Friday signing, identify the entries requiring correction. Who signs after you? What do you do if the email is missing?
4. Fictional structured question: “What did you do today to check that you understood an instruction?” Answer as a student who watched a supervisor demonstrate supply sorting, repeated the steps back and asked where unclear items belong.
5. Write a separate 1–2 sentence reflection about that fictional learning and a next step. Label both answers PRACTICE. Do not invent your own real placement experience.
