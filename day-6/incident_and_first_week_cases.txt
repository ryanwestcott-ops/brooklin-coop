DAY 6 — INCIDENT & FIRST-WEEK CASES
Fictional practice only. Do not enter personal medical details.

1 · Retail injury
You cut your finger while unpacking stock. It seems minor and someone says to finish the task first.

2 · Childcare exposure concern
An unfamiliar bodily-fluid spill is near your work area. You have not been trained to handle it.

3 · Workshop near miss
A poorly stored item falls beside you. Nobody appears hurt.

4 · Unfamiliar equipment
Your usual supervisor is away. A staff member asks you to operate a machine you have never used.

5 · Travel incident
Compare an incident on your ordinary commute with one while carrying out an approved assigned delivery. You are asked whether either is definitely covered.

6 · Delayed concern
After leaving placement, you notice symptoms you think may relate to the day’s work. You did not report anything before leaving.

Complete TWO cases in detail: one from 1–3 and one from 4–6. For each give: immediate safe action; workplace and school contacts; factual information to report; what you must not assume/do; and a 2–3 sentence message. For the other four, give a safe first action and who you would contact.

FIRST-WEEK SIMULATION
A. Your bus is delayed and you will arrive late. Draft a message and identify every required notification.
B. You arrive and the named supervisor is absent. What do you do before beginning any task?
C. Someone asks you to upload a client record into a public AI tool. Give your response and an appropriate way to document learning.
D. Your host asks you to stay beyond your approved schedule. What must happen before you agree?
Write two sentences for each, then improve one answer after rereading the lesson.
