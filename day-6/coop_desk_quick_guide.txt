CO-OP DESK — STUDENT QUICK GUIDE
Brooklin High School · DCO3O9

ARRIVE: Scan your placement QR code and tap Check in when you arrive.
LEAVE: Scan the same QR. Answer the day's structured question and write a 1–2 sentence reflection, then tap Check out.
Use your DDSB Google account in your browser. No app or extra password. Location sharing is optional and does not block attendance.
The reflection is required for checkout. Describe learning honestly without client names, records, photographs or identifying details.
At 5:00, a forgotten checkout closes with estimated hours and an email. Tell the teacher your actual times and any error; estimates need review. If your approved shift goes past 5:00, confirm the arrangement with your teacher before starting that schedule.
ABSENT: Use the teacher-provided absence link, choose Planned or Unexpected, give the requested reason and submit. It emails your teacher. Also notify your placement and follow school attendance procedures; the tool does not contact everyone for you. A scheduled day with no check-in and no absence report is flagged; two in a row alerts the teacher.
FRIDAY: Open your email summary, read all daily entries and total hours, then Sign as student only when accurate. Your supervisor signs next, then your teacher. Report errors before signing; ask your teacher about a missing sign-off email. No additional weekly essay is required.
NO PHONE / DEAD BATTERY / ACCESS ISSUE: Tell your teacher in advance where possible. Arrange the approved alternative. Keep a private temporary note of actual arrival/departure and learning, then report it promptly. Do not ask a friend to check in for you or invent a timestamp.
The QR, absence URL and individual sign-off email come from the teacher/system. Today's lesson does not make live attendance entries.
