## Summative design notes

- **Structure (14 slides in the template, ~11 content):** title, placement, Skill 1, Skill 2, then vs. now, Oct 7 goal, Nov 4 toolkit highlight, Dec 2 Skills for Success, next step, advice and thanks, then a closing slide. The section dividers are optional.
- **Demonstration of learning:** two skills with evidence (photos of the student's own work with permission, checklists, supervisor feedback, Co-op Desk logs, before/after) plus a growth slide.
- **Toolkit:** students copy 1–2 favourite toolkit slides and add an update, per your choice. The full toolkit was already marked on Nov 4.
- **Rubric:** four Achievement Chart categories (K/U, Thinking, Communication, Application), each /10, total /40. Levels use the usual 10-point bands (L4 8–10, L3 7, L2 6, L1 5). Weighting within the final 30% is yours to set. The page says "your teacher sets how this summative counts".
- **Alternative arrangement:** the page tells students who find whole-class presenting a barrier to talk to you early (small group or recorded).
- **Ministry alignment:** the curriculum website could not be reached from the build environment, so no expectation codes are cited. Add them to the instructions Doc if your course outline uses them.
- **Time check:** 5–7 minutes per student plus changeover is roughly 8 minutes each, so about 9 presentations fit in one 75-minute period. For a larger class, plan both periods on January 13, or use small-group rounds.
