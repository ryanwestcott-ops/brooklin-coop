## Teacher notes

Timing totals 150 instructional minutes: 10 start + 20 records + 15 Learn + 15 Check + 15 discuss + 40 reflection + 25 goal/submit + 10 exit. The passing break follows the school's schedule and sits outside the 150 minutes. Mostly self-directed; offer brief individual check-ins during the records and reflection blocks.

Reflection feedback: look for a concrete example, explanation of learning, a relevant course connection, and a next action. Goal feedback: look for feasible approved duties, two actions, support, timeframe and evidence. Exit ticket is a completion/follow-up check; apply the course's existing grading policy.

Students without placement use a labelled supervised school/volunteer/work experience or fictional scenario and set a readiness goal. No invented hours. App corrections and urgent safety concerns are handled directly, not deferred to an exit ticket. New app-specific controls are intentionally not assumed.

Website answers, record checkboxes and eight progress steps save to this lesson’s localStorage key on the same browser/device. A status message reports storage failures; downloads remain usable. Students should clear drafts after submitting on shared devices. Google Doc templates are native, verified and shared as reader with the ddsb.ca domain (not discoverable in search). Force-copy access should be checked with a student account. No responses are sent to or stored in the public repository.

## Added first-return check-in

Verify WEA signatures and coverage in the official records, not from the student checkbox. Follow up immediately on uncertainty or changes. The safety response asks about actual exits/meeting points, first aid, eyewash when relevant, PPE, task instruction and reporting contacts. The sick-day scenario reinforces both employer and school notification before start time and an app absence report where available. Rights/reporting guidance names Mr. Westcott, workplace reporting and an alternate contact if the supervisor is involved.

These are useful integration-day checks; no claim is made that the Ministry mandates this exact first-day sequence.
