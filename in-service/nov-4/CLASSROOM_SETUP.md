## What changed from the old well-being materials

- **Dropped:** Healthy Aging / Dan Buettner "How to live to be 100+" (19 minutes). The talk is long and focused on retirement. Recent research has also questioned the Blue Zones longevity data (birth-record and age-verification errors), so it is weak as a factual source.
- **Optional only:** Magnus Walker, "Go with your gut feeling" (19 minutes) is now an optional at-home link.
- **Perseverance questions:** kept word for word as A1–A4, plus A5, a thought swap. The page frames "positive thinking" as realistic, helpful thinking rather than forced positivity, and growth mindset as effort plus new strategies plus help.
- **Ikigai:** the old worksheet (two Venn images) is replaced by B1–B6, which tie the circles to the placement and next steps. The page notes that the four-circle diagram is a Western adaptation and that in Japan ikigai does not have to be paid work.
- **Daily routines:** the famous-creators blog (The Marginalian) is replaced by four short routine profiles: three fictional and job-based (apprentice, PSW, a co-op student with a poor routine) and Benjamin Franklin's real schedule. The pattern questions are unchanged. The one-week log and the reflection questions are kept.
- **Work–life balance:** the old post was two bare links. It now has a short summary with the CCOHS and MDSC links kept as "read more". (The links could not be rechecked from the build environment on November 3. Click both before class.)
- **Mental health toolkit:** the 5 + 3 slide structure and 20 marks are kept. The rubric levels overlapped (Level 4 was 16–20 and Level 3 was 14–16), so the levels are now 16–20 / 14–15 / 12–13 / 10–11. A title slide and a "My supports" slide were added. Students choose which slides they share.
- **"Open a Word Doc"** is replaced by a Google Doc attached in Classroom.
