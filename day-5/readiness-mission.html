First-Day Readiness Mission.

Collect the facts. Solve the problems. Build a plan you can actually use.

Mission route1 · Choose route2 · Orientation intel3 · Travel test4 · Pack or pause5 · Decision deck6 · Learning mission7 · Export0 of 14 responses started
0–4 minutes

Choose your mission route.

A · Orientation completed/today

Use answers actually provided. Mark anything unanswered “not yet confirmed.”

B · Orientation later

Use verified employer facts and label predictions. Only the workplace can confirm orientation details.

C · Placement pending

Use one realistic workplace type and label all examples “practice.”

This activity does not authorize travel or placement. Do not invent confirmation, contact an employer without teacher agreement, or include signatures/private records.

4–15 minutes

Build the orientation intel board.

For each area, write confirmed, not yet confirmed or not applicable—teacher/workplace confirmed. Add the source and next action.

Reporting entrance/timeSupervisor + backupPermitted dutiesTraining before tasksHazards + controlsEmergency route/meeting pointFirst aid/reportingClothing/PPEBreak/phone rulesPrivacy/photo rules
15–23 minutes

Pass the travel test.

Practice puzzle

A fictional shift starts at 1:00 p.m. The bus trip is 32 minutes. Walking from the stop takes 8 minutes. The student wants a 10-minute arrival buffer. What is the latest planned departure time? Show the steps. A friend offers a ride that “usually takes 20 minutes,” but has not confirmed. Explain how that affects the plan.

23–30 minutes

Pack, confirm or pause.

Sort each item into: prepare now, confirm first, or do not bring/use without permission.

Directions and supervisor roleNotebook for non-private learning notesClosed-toe shoes requested by workplaceSpecialized PPE seen online but not confirmedPersonal earbudsLunch/water based on workplace rulesPersonal AI app for client recordsRequired training proofCamera for workplace photos
30–40 minutes

Work through the first-day decision deck.

1 · No supervisor

You arrive, but your named supervisor is absent. Another employee says, “Just get started wherever.”

2 · New task

You are asked to use equipment you have not discussed or been trained to use.

3 · Schedule change

The workplace asks you to stay later and return earlier tomorrow than the approved schedule.

4 · Finished early

You complete an assigned task and are unsure what to do next.

40–47 minutes

Create your first learning mission.

47–50 minutes

Review, export and submit.

  • Ten orientation items with status, source and next action
  • Travel calculation, real/practice plan and delay message
  • Nine-item gear sort with explanations
  • Four first-day decisions
  • Learning goal, introduction and evidence-based self-status
