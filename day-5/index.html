My in-school work

Complete this independently on the website. No grade, download or Classroom submission is required. If orientation is later, use that workplace. If placement is pending, use an occupation or sector that interests you. Label unknowns and practice answers clearly.

Work from top to bottom. These activities cover two afternoon periods. Ask for help when needed; you do not need to wait for a whole-class lesson. Keep this tab open while you work.

Time: Approx. 35 minutes.

1. Explore two possibilities

Use Job Bank career planning and two workplace websites. If you already have a placement, compare it with another workplace in the same sector. Do not contact employers during this activity.

Time: Approx. 60 minutes.

2. First-Day Readiness Mission

Work through the travel puzzle, gear sort, workplace decisions and introduction. Type your answers and compare them with the feedback. Use the orientation-later or placement-pending route.

Open the self-guided mission ↗

Keep this page open and return here when finished. No file to hand in.

Time: Approx. 25 minutes.

3. Get your questions ready

Read the Orientation Question Card in the booklet above. You are preparing questions, not filling in workplace answers you do not know.

Time: Approx. 20 minutes.

4. Plan and practise

Compare your response

Pause the unfamiliar task. Say what instruction is missing, ask for the designated supervisor or backup, and confirm what you may do safely. Being willing to learn does not mean guessing how to use equipment.

Time: Approx. 10 minutes.

5. Review your work

Reread your answers. Improve one vague response by adding a fact, a specific question or a clear next step.

This is practice only. Nothing is submitted or marked. Do not enter signatures or private workplace records. Your answers remain only while these pages are open.
