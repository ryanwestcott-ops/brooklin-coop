Know your path.

Learning goals

Ask useful orientation questions, record confirmed information and identify what you still need before placement.

Success criteria

  • I can use the booklet to record my workplace arrangements and safety information.
  • I can explain my first-day plan and identify anything still to confirm.
  • I know that only my co-op teacher can confirm clearance to begin placement.

Follow the path assigned by your teacher.

A · Orientation today

Attend only with a confirmed appointment and teacher authorization. Follow your agreed departure, travel and return/debrief arrangements. Complete the student pages with your supervisor.

B · Orientation later

Prepare the booklet for your confirmed workplace. Record what you already know and mark unanswered questions “to confirm.” Complete the Readiness Mission using your B route.

C · Placement pending

Prepare questions for a realistic workplace or sector. Complete the Readiness Mission using your C route and label examples “practice.” Confirm your next placement-search step with your teacher.

If plans change, contact your teacher. A practice answer or completed website checklist does not confirm a workplace arrangement.

What you need to get done today

Use this checklist with your booklet. Check only what you have actually completed. For B or C, leave visit-specific items unchecked until your orientation.

Visit timing: follow your individual appointment and debrief schedule. The 2.5-hour minimum applies to scheduled placement days, not the length of today’s orientation.

Bring to orientation

Orientation Day Package

Your booklet contains the checklists, question card, student response pages, WEA instructions and workplace copies. Bring it to your visit. Ask your supervisor and record real answers; mark unknowns “to confirm.”

Prepare before you go.

Use the Orientation Question Card in your booklet. You will record the answers on the booklet’s student pages.

If any travel or appointment detail is missing, check with your teacher before leaving. If you are on B or C, prepare what you can and leave unconfirmed items unchecked.

Time: Approx. 50 minutes.

Complete the First-Day Readiness Mission.

Work through seven mission stations: orientation intel, travel, gear, workplace decisions, a learning goal and your first-day introduction.

Open the First-Day Readiness Mission

  1. Use your teacher-assigned A, B or C route.
  2. Complete all 14 response boxes. Use confirmed facts, or clearly label practice answers and unknowns.
  3. Download your mission file and attach it to the matching Classroom assignment.

The website does not save or submit your work automatically.

Time: Approx. 5–10 minutes.

First-day plan & readiness check

Use your booklet. Point form is fine. If something is unknown, write “to confirm” and who you will ask. B and C students should identify what is still pending.

Has your co-op teacher explicitly cleared you to begin placement?

This is a personal check. Answers stay only while this page is open; they are not saved or sent to your teacher. Tell your teacher about anything unresolved and keep your confirmed plan in your booklet. This check does not grant placement clearance.

Final check

Tomorrow, Wednesday, September 16: return to school for Day 6 — final preparation. Thursday, September 17 is the planned first placement day, subject to teacher confirmation.
