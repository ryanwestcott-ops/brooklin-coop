MOCK ORIENTATION — PATH B OR C
Fictional practice only. No real workplace approval or training is provided.

CASE: CEDAR COMMUNITY CENTRE
You are preparing for a co-op role helping staff with activity supplies and reception. The fictional orientation note says:
• Report to the reception desk at your school-approved arrival time. Jordan, the program supervisor, will meet you. No backup supervisor is named.
• Initially, observe reception and help organize lightweight supplies after staff instruction. Do not access participant records, use cleaning products or operate equipment until duties, training and authorization have been confirmed.
• Bags sometimes narrow the supply-room walkway. Staff have told students to report obstructions and wait for a safe route; students should not move unknown loads.
• Staff mention an alarm and an outdoor assembly area, but do not identify the route, meeting point or first-aid contact.
• Closed-toe footwear is requested. Other task-specific protection has not been discussed.
• Staff ask you to let reception know if you will be late. The note does not replace your school reporting procedure.
• One employee says, “You can post pictures from activities to show what you learned.” No workplace or school permission has been confirmed.

TASKS (point form is fine)
1. Sort the note into at least four confirmed fictional facts and four unanswered questions. For each unknown, identify who should answer.
2. Choose three questions to ask before starting tasks, and explain your order.
3. Write the words you would use if asked to move a heavy unidentified box while Jordan is away. Explain the safe next action.
4. Explain why the employee’s photo suggestion is not enough. Propose a privacy-safe way to record learning.
5. Give a 45-second mock introduction, aloud to a partner or as a written script: greet the supervisor, state a learning interest and ask your most important unanswered question. Revise one sentence for clarity.

Finish with a readiness verdict: “I still need confirmation of ___ before ___.”
