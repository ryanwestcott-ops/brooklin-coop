0–5 minutes

Your assignment

Brooklin Co-op needs recommendations for three fictional students. Read the profiles and workplace cards. A strong match considers interests, learning goals, practical access, supervision, safety and opportunities to build skills.

Important: There is no single perfect answer. You earn credit for using evidence and identifying what must still be confirmed. These organizations and students are fictional; this activity does not offer or approve a placement.
