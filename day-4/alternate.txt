DAY 4 — APPROVED PRIOR-PROOF ALTERNATE
Complete only after your teacher accepts previous Worker Awareness proof. This activity does not replace missing training.

0–20 minutes: For each fictional case below, identify the hazard, a safe first action and who should address it.

1. School office: A damaged electrical lead is beside a workstation. A student is told to tape it and keep using it.

2. Auto shop: A machine guard is missing, and someone says the task takes only a minute.

3. Childcare: An unfamiliar bodily-fluid spill is near a play area. A student has not received cleanup training.

4. Farm: A heavy crate must be moved from an awkward position. The student is unsure about an approved handling method.

5. Salon: A product has a hazard label, but the student cannot find its SDS or explain the safe procedure.

20–35 minutes: Sort responsibilities. Explain what a worker, supervisor and employer contribute to preventing one case. Connect the rights to know, participate and refuse unsafe work (through the applicable process) to that case.

35–45 minutes: Compare two controls for one case using the hierarchy. Explain why “just wear PPE” is incomplete. Controls must be selected and implemented by qualified workplace staff.

45–55 minutes: Draft a respectful stop-and-ask message. Explain what generic awareness teaches and what you must still learn at the actual workplace. Submit your answers in the alternate Doc, or copy these prompts into a private Doc.
