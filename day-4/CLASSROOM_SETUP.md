## Quick teacher review

1. Check the two official training certificates separately.
2. Read the game records: look at first choices, retries and the student's written explanations. These are student-generated, self-paced records, not verified independent assessments. A fresh attempt resets the local record.
3. Follow up on unsafe first choices or vague explanations. Ask the student to explain one corrected decision using a different task or workplace example. Expect them to pause an unfamiliar task, identify the missing information/control, and name who to ask or report to.
4. Resolve misunderstandings before clearance. Confirm the placement orientation, required paperwork, emergency/reporting arrangements, and workplace/task-specific instruction through the school's normal process. These games do not cover or verify every placement requirement.

Prioritize the online training. Its estimated 60 + 90 minutes may fill the afternoon. The practice activities add about 40–55 minutes; agree on completion time for students who need it. Do not rush official training to finish the games.

The Day 4 exit ticket remains on the website. It is not submitted automatically.
