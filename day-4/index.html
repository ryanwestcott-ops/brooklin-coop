Day 4: Workplace Safety & WHMIS

Learning goals

Recognize hazards, choose stronger controls and find the safety information you need before a task.

Success criteria

  • I can identify possible harm and explain which concern needs attention first.
  • I can use labels and safety data sheets (SDSs) to find information.
  • I can explain when to stop and ask, and prepare specific orientation questions.

Have your Chromebook and headphones ready. Upload each training certificate to its own Classroom assignment. Complete the two interactive challenges here afterwards and upload their completion records to Classroom. Complete the exit ticket on this website.

  1. Complete Worker Health and Safety Awareness and upload your certificate.
  2. Complete WHMIS Training and upload your certificate.
  3. Complete Safety Investigation Casebook and upload your completion record.
  4. Complete Safety & WHMIS Check and upload your completion record.
  5. Complete Day 4 - Exit Ticket on the website.

Training first. The two courses may take the full afternoon. Continue to the activities below after both certificates are uploaded. If you run out of time, tell your teacher and confirm when to finish the remaining activities; do not rush or skip training. Complete the exit ticket at the end of today and identify anything unfinished.

Worker Health and Safety Awareness

  1. Open Ontario Worker Health and Safety Awareness in Four Steps ↗ and choose the worker eLearning program.
  2. Complete the online training and its checks yourself.
  3. Save your official Proof of Completion before closing the training. Check that it opens and shows your name.
  4. Upload the certificate to this Classroom assignment. No worksheet or additional Doc is required.

If your teacher has accepted previous proof, upload that certificate and continue to WHMIS Training.

If training is blocked, unfinished or your certificate is missing, tell your teacher. Do not mark unfinished training complete.

Classroom upload · Ungraded

WHMIS Training

  1. Open AixSafety WHMIS training ↗
  2. Find Launch WHMIS Training. Use headphones and complete the online course and final quiz yourself.
  3. Enter your name as you want it to appear on the certificate. Save or print your certificate as a PDF before leaving the site, then check that it opens.
  4. Upload the certificate to WHMIS Training in Classroom. No worksheet or additional Doc is required.

AixSafety lists approximately 90 minutes for this course. The course and certificate are free; no account or credit card is required. Keep your own certificate copy.

If you cannot launch the training or save the certificate, tell your teacher before closing the page.

General WHMIS education still needs to be followed by workplace-specific instruction about the actual products and tasks you use.

Safety Investigation Casebook

Become a Safety Detective: scan workplace scenes, upgrade weak controls, decode a mystery bottle and practise speaking up. Collect the four stage badges as you work.

Start the interactive challenge →

  1. Work through ten decisions. Read feedback and correct any missed answers to unlock the next step.
  2. Write two short explanations in your own words.
  3. Download your completion record, then upload it to Safety Investigation Casebook in Google Classroom and click Turn in.

No separate Doc or Google Form. This is ungraded practice. The record shows your first choices, retries and explanations for teacher review; it is not an official training certificate or placement clearance.
