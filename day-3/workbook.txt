One full response plan
Choose one case from Set 2. Write a short private report using only the supplied facts: what happened, who was involved by role, what is known/unknown, immediate action, and the help requested. Do not invent dates, quotes or witnesses.

Explain what to do if the supervisor is involved or the first contact does not respond. Include an emergency branch only where immediate danger exists.

Name one respectful behaviour or barrier-removal step that could help prevent this kind of concern. Explain why.
